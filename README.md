# The UNIX-HATERS Handbook - Chapter 1: Unix, The World's First Computer Virus

> Literature avers that Unix succeeded because of its technical superiority.
  This is not true. Unix was _evolutionarily superior_ to its competitors, but
  not _technically superior_. Unix became a commercial success because it was a
  virus. Its sole evolutionary advantage was its small size, simple design, and
  resulting portability.
